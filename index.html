Reading this table: "Listing gain" compares the exchange debut price to the issue price — what a day-one flip would have earned. "Return since listing" compares today's price (CMP) to the listing price — how the stock has actually performed since. A stock can list at a strong premium and still be underwater today, or the reverse.
